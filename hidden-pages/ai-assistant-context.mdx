This page demonstrates a [hidden page](https://www.mintlify.com/docs/organize/hidden-pages) under a dedicated navigation tab.

The tab itself is marked `hidden: true` with `searchable: true`, so this content stays out of the sidebar while remaining available to site search and AI tools.

## What this page is for

Use pages like this for supporting detail you want assistants and search to retrieve, without cluttering primary navigation.
