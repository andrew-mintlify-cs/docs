<Update label="Week of October 3, 2026">

## New features

**Filter legal documents by service!**

The new **Component Demos** product shows a "Filter by service" control on the [Legal documents](/en/v2/demos/legal-documents) page! Pick **All services**, **Compute**, or **Object Storage** to see only the documents that apply to you!

- **Two styles!** Choose between pills on the [Legal documents](/en/v2/demos/legal-documents) page and a compact [dropdown](/en/v2/demos/legal-documents-dropdown)!
- **Shareable views!** Your selection is saved in the page URL, so you can send a filtered list straight to a teammate!
