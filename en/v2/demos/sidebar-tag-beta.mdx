This page shows a second sidebar tag. The label can be any string, not only New.

The **Beta** label next to this page in the sidebar comes from the page `tag` field:

```yaml
---
title: "Beta tag"
sidebarTitle: "Beta tag"
tag: "Beta"
---
```

Use the same field for labels such as Beta, Updated, or Deprecated. The pill stays until you remove `tag`.
