## When tabs help

Use **tabs** when content areas are peers—Guides vs API vs Cookbook—and users switch contexts often. Tabs advertise distinct destinations without nesting everything in the sidebar.

Avoid tabs when you only have two thin pages. In that case a single group or a dropdown is usually clearer.

<Note>
Tabs Product in this starter kit is a **sidebar product** that *contains* horizontal tabs. Do not confuse it with the top-level product switcher.
</Note>
