This second hidden page under **Compare Tabs** captures a lightweight decision guide for CS and solutions engineers.

## Decision framework

Ask three questions before recommending a pattern:

1. **How many top-level areas?** Three or more peer areas favor tabs.
2. **Do users compare side by side?** Prefer a single long page or dropdowns if they need one continuous scroll.
3. **Is there a product boundary?** Use the product switcher (with or without a product menu) when docs sets are truly separate.

<Check>
If the answer to all three points to “peers inside one product,” start with tabs and revisit after user testing.
</Check>

## Phrasing for customers

Say: “Tabs are for switching *modes* of the same product. Dropdowns are for lighter section switching. Product menus are for multi-column entry points from the product switcher.”
