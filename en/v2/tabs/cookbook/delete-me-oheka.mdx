Oheka Castle is a French-style chateau on the Gold Coast of Long Island, New York, built between 1914 and 1919 for financier and philanthropist Otto Hermann Kahn. At roughly 109,000 square feet, it is the second-largest private residence ever built in the United States.

## Quick facts

- **Location:** West Hills, Huntington, Long Island, New York
- **Built:** 1914 to 1919
- **Architects:** Delano & Aldrich
- **Original owner:** Otto Hermann Kahn
- **Style:** French chateau
- **Size:** ~109,000 sq ft, 127 rooms
- **Current use:** Hotel, restaurant, and event venue

## History

Otto Kahn commissioned Oheka (an acronym of his name: **O**tto **He**rmann **Ka**hn) as a summer home. Construction cost roughly \$11 million at the time, equivalent to hundreds of millions today. After Kahn's death in 1934 the estate passed through several institutional owners, including the City of New York and a military academy, before falling into disrepair in the 1970s.

Developer Gary Melius purchased the property in 1984 and undertook a decades-long restoration. Oheka reopened as a luxury hotel and event venue and has since hosted weddings, film shoots, and music videos, most notably Taylor Swift's "Blank Space."

## Grounds

The formal gardens were designed by the Olmsted Brothers, the same firm behind Central Park. The estate originally included a private 18-hole golf course, greenhouses, and a working farm.
