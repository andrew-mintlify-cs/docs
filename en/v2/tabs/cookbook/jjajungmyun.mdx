Jjajangmyeon is a Korean-Chinese noodle dish tossed in a savory black bean sauce made from chunjang, pork, and vegetables. This recipe walks through the ingredients, sauce, and assembly.

## Ingredients

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Sauce" icon="bowl-food">
    Chunjang (fermented black bean paste), pork belly, onion, zucchini, cabbage, potato, sugar, oyster sauce, sesame oil, cornstarch slurry.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Noodles & garnish" icon="wheat-awn">
    Fresh or dried jjajangmyeon noodles, julienned cucumber, danmuji (pickled radish).
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Fry the chunjang">
    Heat 3 tablespoons of neutral oil in a wok. Add 4 tablespoons of chunjang and fry over medium-low heat for 2 to 3 minutes to remove bitterness. Set aside.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cook the pork and vegetables">
    In the same wok, sear diced pork belly until browned. Add diced onion, potato, zucchini, and cabbage. Stir-fry until softened, about 5 minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build the sauce">
    Return the fried chunjang to the wok. Add 1.5 cups of water or stock, 1 tablespoon of sugar, and 1 teaspoon of oyster sauce. Simmer for 5 minutes, then thicken with a cornstarch slurry. Finish with a drizzle of sesame oil.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cook the noodles">
    Boil the noodles according to package directions, drain, and rinse briefly under warm water.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Serve">
    Divide noodles into bowls, ladle sauce on top, and garnish with cucumber. Serve with danmuji on the side.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  For a richer flavor, use a mix of pork belly and shrimp, and let the sauce rest for a few minutes before serving.
</Tip>
